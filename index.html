Before you try it

What this preview can show

The camera tracks your shoulders, hips and legs to place a 2D garment drawing over the video. Stand back so the relevant parts of your body are visible.

It does not determine your size or reproduce fabric texture and drape. Your camera frames are processed in the browser. If you save an image, it downloads to your device.
